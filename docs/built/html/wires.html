Wires

A wire is a cable between two switches: what one of them sends to the other, the wire passes on, both ways. It runs dpipe, which joins two vde_plug, one in each switch.

Ends

Left end, Right end

The switches at the two ends of the wire. Each list has Nothing, then the switches and the switch wrappers of the project, by their name. With Plugs into machines, in the settings of the project, it has the socket cards of the virtual machines too, by the name of their socket: see Network cards and The project.

An end in Nothing keeps the wire from starting: it is Not connected, and the end says so under it, in the colour of warnings. Connect To, in the menu of the wire, and the picture of the Topology tab connect its ends too; see The menu of a brick and Connecting two bricks.

While it runs

New ends take effect when the wire starts again. Restart, under Process in its menu, does it at once; see The process of a brick.

Stopping the wire cuts the link between its switches, and starting it again mends it: A link that goes down and up does it over and over.
