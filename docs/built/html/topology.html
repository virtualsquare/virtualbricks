The picture of the lab

The Topology tab draws the bricks of the project and the links between them. A stopped brick is grey. The brick under the pointer lights up, and its tooltip says its name, its kind and its state. The right button opens the menu of the brick, as in the Bricks tab, and a double click its settings.

Zooming

A bar over the top right corner has Zoom Out, the zoom level, Zoom In, Fit All and a menu. A click on the level goes back to 100%. The tab opens with the whole lab in view, and keeps it in view as the lab changes, until you zoom.

  • Ctrl++, Ctrl+-: zoom in and out; Ctrl+0: 100%; F: fit all.

  • Ctrl and the wheel of the mouse, or a pinch on a touchpad, zoom around the pointer.

  • Dragging the background moves the picture.

Connecting two bricks

Drag a brick onto another, as in the Bricks tab: a line follows the pointer. Over a brick that it can plug into, or that can plug into it, the line ends on that brick, which lights up, and the words under it say what letting go does, as Connect tap1 to sw2. Let go there, and the two connect, as with Connect To in the menu of the brick; let go over another brick, or press Esc, and nothing changes. Near an edge of the tab, the picture scrolls, to reach a brick out of view.

Moving bricks

Drag a brick and let go over free room: the brick stays there. On the way, a faded copy of it at the end of the line shows where it goes. Near the column or the row of another brick, the copy lines up with it, and a thin line shows it.

The first move keeps every other brick where it shows: the lab is laid out by hand from then on, and the project keeps the place of each brick, so the picture stays as you arranged it, after a restart and in an exported archive. A new brick waits in a row under the others until you move it.

Layout and export

The menu of the bar has the Layout: Left to Right or Top to Bottom, where Graphviz places the bricks again after each change, or By Hand, where each brick stays at its place. By Hand gives every brick the place it has in the picture; Left to Right and Top to Bottom take the places away.

In a lab laid out by hand, the links are straight lines, and a line can pass behind a brick in its way. Curved Links, in the menu, has Graphviz route them around the bricks instead, as in the layouts of Graphviz, and moves no brick; the project keeps the choice. Two bricks too close for a link to pass between them have their links routed around their icons, across their names.

The menu also has Export as Image…: a PNG, an SVG or a PDF file of the picture at 100%, on white, whatever the zoom and the theme. Exported to the folder of the project, the picture can show in its README; see The README of a project.
