§1At a glance

Pictures
Eleven, one for each page about a window: the Bricks tab of the first lab, New Brick, the settings of a switch, of a machine and of an event, the Images, Topology and Readme tabs, the Projects window, Export and Settings (§3, F1).
Taken by
Scenarios of their own, in e2e/features/pictures.feature: each makes the lab of its picture with the steps there are, then Then the picture settings shows the Settings window (§5).
A picture
The window’s own surface, as broadwayd sent it: the window as GTK drew it, its shadow transparent, the menus open over it, no pointer. The same run gives the same bytes (§4, F3).
Updating
pytest --pictures writes them into help/C/figures/. Any other run takes them into the folder of the scenario, and its summary lists those that differ, without failing: fonts and GTK differ by machine (§5, F2).
The guide
A Mallard <figure> in each page; yelp-build copies the pictures into help/html/C/figures/; installed beside the pages and the HTML; on the website through sync-docs. In Git LFS, as the other PNGs (§6, F4).
Plan
Three steps on develop, a commit each, then one on website (§9).

§2Today

  • The guide has no pictures. Page 28 left them for later (§12 there): the sixteen pages of help/C/ are text.
  • The end-to-end tests already keep the screen. The browser of e2e/broadway.py records the timeline of broadwayd: each surface as it is made, its images, and after each change the surfaces that show. e2e/recording.py replays it into the screenshot and the video of a scenario that fails: the whole screen, 1280 × 1024, with a red dot for the pointer and the step as a caption.
  • A window is a surface. In GTK 3 on Broadway each window is a surface of its own, with its client-side title bar and its shadow inside it; a menu is another surface, a popover is drawn in its window’s.
  • The PNGs are in Git LFS on develop (*.png in .gitattributes), the twelve icons of virtualbricks/gui/data/ among them. git archive, which sync-docs uses, gives the images themselves, not the pointers.
  • yelp-build html copies the pictures a page shows into figures/ of its output. help/build.py compares only the top of help/html/C/, and the help hook runs on a change to a page or to build.py, not to a picture.

Checked today with a prototype step in a scratch worktree, on Debian 13.6, GTK 3.24.49 and broadwayd: a picture of the main window with sw1, and of the Settings window. Two runs gave the same bytes, 33 KB and 55 KB. A crop of the screen by the window’s extents, as AT-SPI gives them, takes the margin of its shadow and what shows behind it; the surface itself is clean (§4). GDK_SCALE=2 changes nothing on GTK 3’s Broadway: the pictures are at scale 1. The fonts are this machine’s: DejaVu Sans; Cantarell isn’t installed.

§3The pictures

A picture for each page whose subject is a window, at the place the page first names it. The lab of each is small and the same where it can be: sw1, and vm1 plugged into it, in the project new_project of the default workspace, so the title names only the project.

§4Taking a picture

  1. The step finds the window through AT-SPI, as the other steps do, and the surface of broadwayd at its place and of its size.
  2. It moves the pointer off the windows, so that nothing shows hovered, and waits until the screen hasn’t changed for half a second.
  3. It replays the timeline to that moment, as recording.py does for a screenshot, without the pointer or a caption.
  4. The picture is the surface’s image: the window as GTK drew it, with its client-side shadow, which stays transparent. A menu open over the window, a surface of its own, is drawn where it shows, on a canvas that holds both.
  5. cairo writes it as a PNG.

Nothing in the pictures may change from one run to the next: no time, no process number, no path of the scenario’s folder. The title of a window in another workspace names it, as ~/workspace, so the scenarios of the pictures run in the default one.

§5Keeping them current

  • A scenario for each picture, in e2e/features/pictures.feature, run with the others. It makes its lab with the steps there are, then takes the picture with one step of the screen: Then the picture settings shows the Settings window. A picture that needs QEMU is tagged as the scenarios of machines are, and skipped without it.
  • pytest --pictures, a new option of e2e/conftest.py, writes each picture into help/C/figures/NAME.png. The help hook then rebuilds the HTML, as for a change to a page.
  • Any other run writes the picture into the folder of the scenario, and the end of the run lists the pictures that differ from those of help/C/figures/. It doesn’t fail: the pictures carry the fonts and the GTK of the machine that takes them, so on another machine every picture differs (F2).
  • When a window changes, its scenario still runs, and the list says which picture to take again; a scenario that no longer runs fails as any other.

§6Into the guide

  • In a page, a Mallard figure, with a title and a text for those who don’t see it. Yelp shows the picture; the HTML has it as an <img>.
  • help/build.py compares figures/ too, and the hook runs on a change to help/C/figures/ as well.
  • Installed beside what they belong to: share/help/C/virtualbricks/figures/ and share/virtualbricks/help/C/figures/, a key of data-files each, as now. MANIFEST.in takes them.
  • Git LFS takes them as it takes every PNG. The picture in help/C/figures/ and its copy in help/html/C/figures/ are the same file, so LFS keeps one object for both (F4). A clone without git-lfs gets pointers in place of pictures, as it already does for the icons.
  • The website gets them with the next invoke sync-docs, which copies help/html/C/ with its subfolders; the website branch keeps PNGs in LFS too.
  • Size: about 40 KB a picture, so 450 KB for the eleven, in the tree twice and in LFS once.

§7Other directions

  • Pictures by hand, with a screenshot tool on the desktop. Nothing to build, but each one shows the desktop’s theme and fonts, and they drift from the windows as nobody takes them again.
  • A crop of the screen by the window’s extents, as AT-SPI gives them: simple, but it takes the margin of the shadow and what shows behind the window, as the prototype found.
  • Xvfb with GDK_SCALE=2: pictures sharp on high-resolution screens, which Broadway in GTK 3 can’t make. It needs Xvfb, which the end-to-end tests don’t use yet, and a window manager or none at all; for later (§11).
  • A check that fails on a picture that differs: on the machine that took them it is exact, on any other it fails every time, for fonts alone.

§8The code and the tests

What changes

  • e2e/harness.py: Virtualbricks.picture(window, path): the pointer away, the screen quiet, the replay.
  • e2e/conftest.py: --pictures, and the list of the pictures that differ at the end of a run.
  • e2e/steps.py, e2e/STEPS.md: the step of the picture; e2e/features/pictures.feature; e2e/README.md: how to take them again.
  • help/C/*.page: the figures; help/C/figures/ and help/html/C/figures/; help/build.py and the hook.
  • pyproject.toml, MANIFEST.in, the README.

Tests

  • picture() on a timeline made by hand, in e2e/test_recording.py: a surface, a menu over it, a surface that doesn’t show.
  • The guide against its pictures, in virtualbricks/tests/test_help.py: each <media> names a file of help/C/figures/, each picture there is shown by a page, the HTML has a copy of each, and pyproject.toml installs them.
  • The scenarios of the pictures run twice in a row with the same bytes.
