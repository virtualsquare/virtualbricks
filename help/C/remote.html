Virtualbricks Help › Settings and beyond

Another computer

The bricks can run on one computer, the lab machine, and the windows on another, your desktop. The lab machine runs Virtualbricks with --listen, with its own windows or without, and your desktop opens its windows with --connect. Both run the same version of Virtualbricks.

# on the lab machine
virtualbricks --no-gui --no-term --listen \
    'ssl:8765:interface=0.0.0.0:privateKey=~/vb/lab.key'
# on your desktop
virtualbricks --connect 'ssl:lab.example:8765:caCertsDir=~/vb/lab'

A connection over the network proves that it knows the token of the lab machine, the file ~/.config/virtualbricks/token, so copy it to your desktop; or the lab machine asks for the certificate of your desktop instead. virtualbricks --connect alone opens the windows of a Virtualbricks of yours on this computer, started with --listen.

How to listen and connect, the token and the certificates are in virtualbricks(1), and the protocols in virtualbricks-control(7).

What the windows do

  • They follow the project open there, whoever changes it, and what you do goes there: a start, a new brick, the OK of the settings of a brick.

  • What they show is the lab machine's: its QEMU programs, its USB devices, its files and its projects. A path is a path there, typed with the completion of its folders.

  • The consoles of the bricks open in a terminal of your desktop.

  • Settings has a page for your desktop, one for the lab machine, and one for the project.

  • Import, export, the long work on images and Show in Files wait for a later version, greyed.

When the connection is lost, a bar says why, and Reconnect connects again. Quit closes the windows: the Virtualbricks on the lab machine goes on.
