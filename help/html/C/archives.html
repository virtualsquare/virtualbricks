Import and export

An archive, a .vbp file, holds a project, to copy it to another computer, send it to a student, or keep it.

The format of the archives is in virtualbricks-vbp(5).

Exporting a project

Choose Projects ▸ Export…, or Export… in the Projects window. The window asks where to save the archive and what to put in it, each with its size:

  • the project, its file and its README, always;

  • the private copies of the disks of its machines, on at first;

  • the images, off at first, since they are often large and the other computer may have them;

  • the other files of the folder of the project.

The export runs in the background, with its progress. You can stop it, or close the window and let it finish: the Logs window says when it's done. With qemu-img installed, the disks are compressed, and stay disks that QEMU can run.

Importing a project

Choose Projects ▸ Import…, or Import… in the Projects window, then the archive. One page shows what it holds:

Name

The name of the new project: the one in the archive, or the next free one if it is taken. An import never replaces a project.

Images

For each image of the project: Copy it from the archive to the shared images, Use a file… of this computer, or Leave unset, for its disks to get an image later. Each has a choice made already, so Import works at once.

This computer's paths

The folders of QEMU and VDE of the project are replaced with yours when they don't exist here.

Open the project after the import

On at first.

If you stop an import, or it fails, it removes what it wrote. At the end, the Logs window says what needs your attention, as an image left unset. The archives of Virtualbricks 2.1 import too.
